# WE ARE THE DJs
## Interactive performance art environment

The three environments represent three stages of the world’s existence: the natural order, human turmoil, and post-apocalyptic ruin. Three controllers allow three people to interact with the space simultaneously. There are no instructions, rules, or predetermined goals.

The work, inspired primarily by Marina Abramović’s *Rhythm 0*, Zbigniew Rybczyński’s *Tango*, and the work of Theo Triantafyllidis, explores the moment when free interaction turns into a need for domination. Initially, participants cautiously test the relationship between a gesture and the environment’s reaction. However, the situation quickly begins to spiral out of control. Full agency, the absence of instructions, and the presence of others lead not to harmony, but to escalation, overload, and chaos.

This is an open-ended work. The several-dozen-second excerpt from the recording of the first version shown here is merely the beginning of the process. The number of environments, interactions, and participants in this situation can be further expanded. The project is designed for continuous expansion, in which each subsequent element increases the system’s complexity and intensifies the tension between control and collapse.
